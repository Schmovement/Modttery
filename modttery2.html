How It Works

Six steps. Zero tools. Full intelligence.

01

Build Your Power

Insert modules into the tray — they click into place magnetically.

02

Smart Connection

The BMS auto-detects modules, reads status, and balances cells instantly.

03

Power Flow

Voltage, current, and temperature monitored in real time across all modules.

04

Charging

USB-C PD 3.1 up to 240W. BMS manages safe optimized charging per module.

05

Safe & Protected

Guards against overcharge, overdischarge, short circuit, and overtemperature.

06

Upgrade Anytime

Add or swap modules on the fly. The system adapts automatically.
